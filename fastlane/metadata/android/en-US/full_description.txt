Muscle memory for your phone.

Flyt is a calm, one-handed Android launcher. Press where your thumb already is, drag toward a direction and let go. Learn a direction once and your thumb just goes there, the way your fingers know a keyboard shortcut.

THREE WAYS FORWARD FROM HOME
• Radial: each direction holds an app, an action like the flashlight, or a tag whose apps unfold in a second ring.
• Search: apps, contacts, actions and app shortcuts as you type.
• Apps: a predictable list with local, overlapping tags and tag slots under your thumb.

MADE FOR YOUR HAND
Left or right hand, half or full circle, five to eight directions, start anywhere or from a fixed spot. Guidance fades as you learn.

THEMES
Flyt ships with Blåtime, four Nordic blue-hour scenes with a single warm light, and Krets, plus palettes like Tokyo Night, Gruvbox and Catppuccin. Make a theme from your own photos, or paste the GitHub link of any Omarchy theme.

What the keyboard is to Omarchy, your thumb is to Flyt.

QUIET BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No analytics. Your settings and what Flyt learns stay on your phone. Flyt only goes online when you ask it to fetch a theme from GitHub.

OPEN SOURCE
Flyt is free software under GPL-3.0. The same app is free on GitHub; buying it here gets you automatic updates and supports development.
